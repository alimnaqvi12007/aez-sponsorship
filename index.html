The team

Student engineers. A national stage.

Aatish-e-Zarrar is a student-led engineering team preparing for Race Pakistan 2027. Organised by NUVEX, Race Pakistan is a national STEM competition where student teams design, manufacture, present and race miniature formula-style cars.

Teams take on the whole project: vehicle design, manufacturing, business planning, presentations and racing. We are building the car, the business case and the story around it, and we are looking for partners who want to be part of that work.

    Before you decide

    Questions sponsors ask.

    How will my contribution be used?

    Contributions go into the car and the campaign: materials and parts, manufacturing, software, printing and transport. We commit to using contributions responsibly and to showing you how they were used in our final sponsor report.

    When will my brand appear?

    Digital recognition begins once your sponsorship is confirmed. Logos on the car and the team jersey follow Race Pakistan's competition rules and need approval, so we confirm the exact placement with you once that is settled.

    What will we receive after the event?

    A final sponsor report that covers the project, how the team performed and how your support was recognised. Higher packages also include engineering progress updates while we build.

    Can we support with products or services instead of money?

    Yes. The Fuel partnership is for materials, parts, equipment, manufacturing or technical support, software, printing and transport. Recognition is agreed with you and scales with the value of what you provide.

    Can you build a custom package?

    Yes. Tell us what you would like to support and what kind of visibility suits your organisation, and we will propose terms.

    How do we confirm a sponsorship?

    Send us an enquiry using the form below or reach us directly. We will confirm the package with you, share payment details and begin your recognition.
